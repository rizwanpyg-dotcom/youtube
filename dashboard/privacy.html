Privacy Policy

Applies to the automation tool ("the tool") that manages a single YouTube channel for its owner.

What the tool is

The tool is a private, single-user automation. It researches public sources, produces original videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It also reads that channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API. It has no other users and offers no sign-in to the public.

Data accessed

  • The owner's own YouTube channel: the ability to upload and update its videos, thumbnails and captions, and to read its analytics (views, watch time, subscribers).
  • Public web pages and public datasets used as research sources.

Data not collected

The tool doesn't collect, store or process personal data about viewers or any other YouTube users. It doesn't read comments or viewer identities, and it doesn't sell or share any data.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never written to logs, the database or this website. Aggregated channel statistics are stored in the tool's database for performance analysis. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
